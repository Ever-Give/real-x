So this JUST happened. I drank less than my normal amount of tee this morning because I felt like going for a long jog / hike without having to worry about finding somewhere to pee. It was a bit chilly, so I wore some compression socks, some gray running pants, black shorts along with a compression shirt and hoodie. I peed before leaving the house and the only thing I did before setting out on my jog was take the trash bins in and toss out some recyclables in the containers.

I set off on my run and was feeling better than expected, as it’s been waaaay too long!!! That was the first 8 minutes 🙂 it was shortly after that where I thought to myself out loud, “how is it possible that I have to pee?”. It was at that point where I should have abandoned my run… a few minutes later my urge to pee shot up exponentially and I felt some spurts escape with each stride. Thankfully they stopped but I could not immediately assess the damage because of the cars and people around. I kept jogging until I could find a suitable place to see what others could see… was by a cemetery on one side and an open field on the other …..

&&

So it was a little worse than I thought and still had about 25 minutes home in that direction. At this point there are some walkers heading my way, so did my best to pull my hoodie down and keep going. Walking here and there seemed to help, but spurts were still escaping which also helped… I’m a bit surprised because once I start, I usually can’t stop…. Regardless, I found a place to stop again and snap a photo. The wet spot was a bit bigger and had some evidence on my hoodie from when I tried to cover it up…

&&

With only about 300-400 meters to go, I was VERY desperate!!! The feeling where no matter what method you attempt, the flood gates are going to open at any second…. Well I made it to the start of my street and had no choice but to sprint the last 100-150 meters because I completely lost the battle… I didn’t even realize it was possible to pee that much while running. Cars passing by, DHL dude, a person in their yard…. Finally I made it home… I was absolutely freaking SOAKED!!!!
